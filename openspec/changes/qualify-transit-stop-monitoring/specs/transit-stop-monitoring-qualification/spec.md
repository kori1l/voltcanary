## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Generic capability ownership replaces specialized implementation

The retained transit scenarios SHALL be implemented through Cross-domain qualification (G8); transit is one retained fixture family. This superseded delta SHALL NOT introduce transit-only core tables, an independent engine/ledger or a hardcoded required-stop condition type. Normative VCL/storage/import/adapter contracts apply; old scenario semantics remain acceptance constraints rather than a separate implementation queue.

#### Scenario: Transit scenario is implemented
- **WHEN** a team implements the retained HSL example
- **THEN** it uses the current generic capability owners and actual source evidence, preserving mandatory policy and unknownness without reviving T1–T7 as separate work



### Requirement: Independent complete-task acceptance

Qualification SHALL grade source-linked expected effects, exact reviewed/saved resources and event outcomes independently of model explanations, with frozen negative and adversarial scenarios.

#### Scenario: Wrong target
- **WHEN** a well-formed plan monitors another bus or stop
- **THEN** the complete task fails despite passing schemas

#### Scenario: Unreached activation
- **WHEN** a trial fails during source connection
- **THEN** the scenario remains failed with later requested steps recorded as unreached

### Requirement: Separate evidence classes and quality accounting

Reports SHALL separate deterministic fixtures, real-feed smoke, real-model first-pass, recovered/final success, evaluator work and every attempted call/token/cost.

#### Scenario: Recovered success
- **WHEN** repair rescues a failed first candidate
- **THEN** first-pass remains failed while recovered and final success are reported separately

#### Scenario: Unknown price
- **WHEN** a candidate has no usable price snapshot
- **THEN** the report marks monetary cost unknown and makes no savings claim

### Requirement: Runtime resilience and privacy gates

Release SHALL require no false service alerts for UNKNOWN/EXEMPT cases, no duplicate logical alerts on replay/correction, durable restart behavior and no cross-account evidence or unauthorized activation.

#### Scenario: Browser closed and restart
- **WHEN** the evaluator is restarted while all clients are closed
- **THEN** state/timers resume and one eligible alert is produced

#### Scenario: Outage
- **WHEN** HSL/capture evidence is missing during a stop window
- **THEN** the result is UNKNOWN and no service violation notification is sent
