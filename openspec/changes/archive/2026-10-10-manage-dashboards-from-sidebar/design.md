## Approach
The Dashboards parent link targets /dashboards, the existing compact list. Its children open individual published dashboards or unpublished editors. The sidebar contains links only. Group visibility and creation commands reside in the catalog. There is no additional catalog drawer or sidebar search field. Item actions in the list reuse DashboardManagement for default, favorites and idempotent copy. Fetch current permissions and etag for archive/restore; confirm archive. Hidden items are excluded from sidebar only and restored through the catalog Hidden filter. Store expanded/hidden preferences locally per authenticated user in a typed helper, with an in-memory fallback. Keep /dashboard as the personal startup/default route and preserve viewer runtime settings.
