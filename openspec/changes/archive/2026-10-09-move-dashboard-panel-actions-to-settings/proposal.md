## Why
The card's ellipsis opens a bulky floating form for layout and management actions, obscuring the dashboard. These controls belong in the existing right settings drawer.
## What Changes
- Remove the card ellipsis and move layout, screen/section, duplicate and delete controls into a Layout section in panel settings.
- Keep pointer grid editing and keyboard alternatives; allow managing unsupported panels through settings.
## Capabilities
### New Capabilities
- `dashboard-panel-settings-actions`: Central panel layout and management controls.
### Modified Capabilities
## Impact
Frontend editor/settings components and workflow docs. No API or saved definition changes.
